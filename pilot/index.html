CROWRULES STUDENT MEDIA ACADEMY · PILOT PROGRAM

Six-Week School Pilot

Start small. Learn together. Improve with evidence.

A suggested first pilot for one partner school, approximately 10–20 students, and a six-week educator-supervised project cycle. Final scope, staffing, equipment, and permissions would be agreed with the participating school.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Week 1 · Introduction to media

Explore podcast formats, communication, audience, media literacy, and project expectations.

Deliverable: topic idea and project plan.

02 · Week 2 · Research and fact-checking

Identify reliable sources, evaluate evidence, record citations, and distinguish fact from opinion.

Deliverable: research notes and source list.

03 · Week 3 · Writing and planning

Develop a script, interview questions, outline, or storyboard appropriate to the project.

Deliverable: educator-reviewed production plan.

04 · Week 4 · Recording and production

Practice audio or video recording, teamwork, presentation, and production roles.

Deliverable: private first draft.

05 · Week 5 · Editing and revisions

Review accuracy, clarity, sound, visuals, and feedback; make improvements.

Deliverable: revised project.

06 · Week 6 · Presentation and evaluation

Complete a reflection and educator assessment; public sharing only if separately approved.

Deliverable: final project, reflection, assessment, and Academy recognition.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
