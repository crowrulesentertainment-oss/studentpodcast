CROWRULES STUDENT MEDIA ACADEMY · SCHOOL PARTNERSHIPS

For Schools & Educators

A free media learning program, shaped with educators.

CrowRules is developing a school-sponsored educational experience for middle school and high school communities. The goal is to complement classroom learning—not replace district curriculum, school policies, or educator judgment.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · What schools receive

Free core curriculum, six learning pathways, assignment library, educator resources, private project workflow, assessment tools, completion recognition, and aggregate participation reports.

No required school or student participation fees.

02 · What schools oversee

School authorization, teacher supervision, student enrollment, class assignments, grading and credit decisions, required permissions, accessibility arrangements, and publishing approval.

CrowRules certificates document program achievement; they do not independently award school credit.

03 · Academic flexibility

Projects may be considered by a school for extra credit, enrichment, independent study, or review for an elective or credit pathway.

Only the school or its authorized educators decide whether and how a project counts academically.

04 · How to begin

Start with a conversation about learning goals, age groups, staff capacity, privacy review, technology needs, and a supervised pilot.

Initial outreach may begin with Tacoma and Pierce County schools; no district partnership is implied until formally agreed.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
