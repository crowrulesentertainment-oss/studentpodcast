CrowRules Student Media Awards

Recognize the work. Celebrate the learning.

A future annual showcase can recognize educational media projects produced through approved school participation. Student entry should remain free, and public participation should always be optional.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Potential award categories

Best Educational Podcast · Best Student Documentary · Outstanding Student Journalist · Best Science Production · Best Historical Production · Outstanding Audio Producer · Best Collaborative Project · Media Design · Student Media Leadership · School of the Year.

Categories can evolve with participating schools and pathways.

02 · Fair and responsible judging

Publish criteria, use appropriate independent judges, disclose conflicts, and ensure accessible submission options.

Schools verify eligibility and required permissions before public judging or showcase.

03 · Event sponsorship

A presenting partner, category sponsors, event underwriting, and equipment donations can help fund the annual event.

Sponsors do not influence judging, select students, or receive private student information.

04 · Recognition options

Certificates, trophies, school recognition, and optional public showcase opportunities.

No required tickets, merchandise, or paid entry.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
