Safety, Privacy & Responsible Publishing

Private by default. School-guided at every step.

The current Student Podcast Hub is informational only. Student accounts, uploads, and submissions are not open. The following principles are requirements for any future student-facing service, not a claim that legal or school reviews are complete.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · School-sponsored access

Students participate only through an authorized school or approved educational program. No independent student registration is planned.

Schools control enrollment and educator assignments.

02 · Minimum necessary information

Collect only information needed to provide the approved educational service. Keep academic records separate from public creator identities.

No public student directory or unrestricted student-to-student messaging.

03 · Private work and publishing

Student submissions remain private. Public release requires educator review, school authorization, platform review, and all required permissions.

Submitting or completing an assignment never automatically authorizes publication.

04 · Minors and guardian permissions

Age-appropriate design, school agreements, guardian consent where required, and additional safeguards must be reviewed before any real student use.

The optional creator pathway uses separate eligibility and onboarding.

05 · Data access and security

Use role-scoped access, private storage, strong authentication, audit trails, retention and deletion processes, and security reviews.

No student data, academic performance, or private recordings are sold or offered to sponsors.

06 · Compliance and accessibility review

Before launch, obtain appropriate review for FERPA, COPPA, Washington student privacy requirements, accessibility, retention, vendor agreements, and school policies.

These obligations must be validated for the actual service and participating schools.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
