CROWRULES STUDENT MEDIA ACADEMY · MEASURE WHAT MATTERS

Program Impact

Learning outcomes before vanity metrics.

The Academy should report its progress transparently using aggregate, non-identifying information. Impact reporting should show educational participation, completed work, skill development, and responsible program growth.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · School reach

Number of participating districts, schools, educators, and approved classes.

Report only aggregate figures and only when a real pilot or program is operating.

02 · Learning progress

Modules completed, assignments submitted, competencies demonstrated, and certificates earned.

Do not publish individual student grades or academic records.

03 · Creative production

Projects completed, production hours, subject areas represented, and school-approved public showcases.

Private work remains private and is not sponsor content.

04 · Educator and student experience

Feedback collected through school-approved processes, accessibility improvements, and program changes made in response.

Use appropriate consent and avoid unnecessary identifiable information.

05 · Financial sustainability

Sponsorships, funded contracts, commercial service revenue, operating costs, and restricted funding obligations.

Distinguish targets and projections from actual received revenue.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
