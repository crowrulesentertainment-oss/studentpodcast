Partners & Supporters

Help keep creative education free.

Organizations can help fund the Academy while students and schools continue to access the core educational program without participation fees. Partnerships should support learning—not buy access to students or influence over academic decisions.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Corporate supporters

Businesses can provide annual financial support and receive appropriate recognition on supporter pages, impact reports, and approved program materials.

Illustrative levels: Community Supporter $500 · Education Partner $1,500 · Program Sponsor $3,500 · Founding Corporate Partner $7,500 · Presenting Partner $15,000 per year.

02 · Pathway sponsors

Organizations can support podcasting, journalism, digital production, creative writing, or equipment needs.

Sponsors do not select students, access student submissions, control curriculum, or influence assessments.

03 · Equipment and in-kind partners

Microphones, headphones, cameras, lighting, editing equipment, software access, and educator workshop support can strengthen the program.

All donated items and services are subject to school approval and program needs.

04 · Community and institutional partners

Libraries, colleges, foundations, workforce organizations, and community groups may help develop learning opportunities or fund approved delivery.

Funding terms, eligibility, procurement, and restrictions must be documented.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
