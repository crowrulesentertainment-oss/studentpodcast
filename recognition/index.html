Recognition & Academic Alignment

Recognize skills. Respect school authority.

The Academy can provide completion certificates, pathway achievements, portfolio records, and evidence of demonstrated skills. Academic recognition remains a decision for the school.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Extra credit

Teachers may choose to assign Academy work as extra-credit opportunities under their own class policies and grading practices.

CrowRules does not set point values or guarantee extra credit.

02 · Enrichment

Students can complete additional projects to explore a topic, practice a skill, or extend classroom learning.

Enrichment participation can be recognized without changing a course grade.

03 · Independent study

A school may choose to use Academy modules and projects within an approved independent-study arrangement.

The school defines supervision, scope, learning objectives, assessment, and credit.

04 · Formal credit pathway review

Districts may evaluate alignment with communications, journalism, media arts, English language arts, or career and technical education.

No course credit or credential is promised by CrowRules.

05 · Program certificates

Certificates can record pathway completion, project milestones, and demonstrated competencies.

Certificates are Academy achievement records, not transcripts, licenses, or school-issued credits.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
