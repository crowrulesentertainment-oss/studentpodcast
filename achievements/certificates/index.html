Certificates & Educator Recognition

Four optional recognition types

Participation

Recognizes participation in a school-sponsored Academy activity or learning sequence. It does not imply completion or mastery.

Module Completion

Recognizes completion of educator-selected learning activities and required evidence for a module.

Skill Achievement

Recognizes demonstrated evidence against clearly stated skill criteria in one or more competencies.

Capstone Recognition

Recognizes completion and educator review of an integrated final project, supporting evidence, and reflection.

Suggested educator review sequence

  1. Confirm the learner completed the school-sponsored activity or project.
  2. Review evidence against criteria shared in advance.
  3. Confirm accessibility accommodations and equivalent evidence options were honored.
  4. Document feedback and any revisions required by the educator.
  5. School decides whether the recognition is appropriate and how it is recorded.
  6. Issue an Academy certificate only if the school has approved the process and sharing of the student's name.

Certificate of [Participation / Completion / Skill Achievement / Capstone Recognition]

This recognition is presented to

[Student name — completed by the school]

for [activity, module, competency, or capstone title]

in the school-sponsored CrowRules Student Media Academy learning experience.

Recognition date: [Date] · School: [School-approved name]

This document recognizes Academy participation or demonstrated learning evidence. It is not an accredited certification and does not award academic credit. Grades, credit, and transcript decisions remain with the school or district.

Recognition fairness principles

  • Use clear criteria and evidence; do not award based on popularity, views, likes, or public reach.
  • Do not require students to publish work or share personal social profiles.
  • Do not make awards contingent on buying equipment, software, or services.
  • Provide equivalent opportunities for students with disabilities and students who choose non-recording formats.
  • Keep certificate records and student names under school control.
  • Sponsors do not select winners, access student work, or influence curriculum or assessment.

Certificate governance

This is a template, not an active certificate issuance system. Do not enter student names into this public website. The school must authorize any use, confirm the accuracy of the recognition, and retain its own records.
