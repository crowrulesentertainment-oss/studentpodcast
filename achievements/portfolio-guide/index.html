A portfolio is evidence of learning—not a public profile

Use this guide to organize a school-held record of a project's process. The school selects its approved storage and access rules. Do not upload student work to this website or share portfolio links with sponsors.

Suggested portfolio evidence checklist

  • Project pitch: purpose, audience, guiding question, and intended outcome.
  • Learning goals: skills or course outcomes selected by the educator.
  • Research record: source list, notes, claim checks, and citations where relevant.
  • Script or storyboard: outline, draft, interview questions, or visual plan.
  • Ethics and safety checklist: privacy, consent, copyright, fairness, and school approval.
  • Production plan: roles, milestones, equipment/tools approved by school, and accessibility plan.
  • Draft or final artifact: media file, written work, design, storyboard, or non-recording equivalent.
  • Feedback and revision log: educator/peer feedback and changes made.
  • Reflection: what was learned, challenges, contributions, and next steps.
  • Educator assessment: criteria used, feedback, and school-controlled recognition decision.

Suggested portfolio organization

01 · Plan

Pitch, learning goals, audience, roles, and project schedule.

02 · Research

Sources, notes, verification, and evidence of responsible attribution.

03 · Create

Script, storyboard, draft, production notes, or alternative artifact.

04 · Review

Ethics review, accessibility checks, feedback, and revision log.

05 · Reflect

Final reflection, skills demonstrated, and future learning goals.

Educator review and fairness guidance

  • Share criteria before work begins and apply them consistently.
  • Assess the learning objective—not a student's access to a studio, camera, or paid software.
  • Offer accessible formats and reasonable accommodations, including written, visual, oral, and non-recording options.
  • Assess individual contributions fairly in team projects.
  • Do not require public posting, personal social media, or a student's face/voice to be included.
  • Keep feedback constructive, specific, and focused on demonstrated work.
  • Store records only in school-approved systems and follow school retention/deletion policies.

Do not include unnecessary personal information

Use school-approved identifiers and storage practices. Avoid personal contact details, home addresses, private family information, sensitive disclosures, or unnecessary images/recordings. CrowRules does not need copies of student portfolios to provide this framework.
