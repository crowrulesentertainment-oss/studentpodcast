THE BIG IDEA

Not just learning about media.
Learning by creating it.

The CrowRules Student Podcast Hub is being planned as a dedicated educational environment for middle-school, junior-high, and high-school communities. It brings together communication, research, writing, audio production, media literacy, teamwork, and creative confidence.

Students learn how a production works—from the first idea to a polished classroom project—while teachers and school partners shape the experience around their learning goals.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

Student privacy isn't an add-on.
It's the foundation.

This initiative is being designed around school oversight, age-appropriate experiences, and strong privacy boundaries. The current site is informational only and does not collect student information.

  • ✓
    Private by defaultStudent work stays private in the planned experience.
  • ✓
    Educator-guidedTeachers and school partners guide learning and review.
  • ✓
    No open student messagingNo public student directory or unrestricted contact tools.
  • ✓
    School-approved publishingPublic sharing requires a future, reviewed approval process.
  • ✓
    Separate student recordsStudent data is not part of general Podcasting profiles.

Design goals only—not a claim of legal compliance or a currently operating student service. School, privacy, and security reviews must be completed before any real student use.
