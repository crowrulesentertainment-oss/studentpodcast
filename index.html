ONE VISION · THREE CONNECTED PARTS

Education first.
Creative futures next.

Student Media Academy is the educational division. Student Podcast Hub is its technology platform. The Student-to-Creator Pathway is an optional next step for graduates who want to explore CrowRules Podcasting.

The Academy is designed to stay free for schools and students. CrowRules sustains the program through organizations that want to support media education: sponsors, funded partnerships, grants where eligible, and commercial production services.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

Student privacy isn't an add-on.
It's the foundation.

The program is planned around school-sponsored participation, educator oversight, age-appropriate experiences, private student work, and school-approved publishing. The current site is informational only and does not collect student information.

Read the safety and privacy framework →
  • ✓
    School-sponsored accessNo independent student registration.
  • ✓
    Private by defaultStudent work remains private unless properly approved.
  • ✓
    Educator-guidedTeachers and school partners guide learning and review.
  • ✓
    No sponsor access to studentsNo student data, private submissions, or academic records are sold or shared as sponsor benefits.

These are design commitments, not a claim that a student service is currently operating or that legal compliance reviews are complete.
