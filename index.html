THE IDEA

More than a podcast.
A learning experience.

The CrowRules Student Podcast Hub is being planned as a dedicated educational environment for middle-school, junior-high, and high-school communities. Students can build communication, research, storytelling, teamwork, and media-literacy skills through guided projects.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

Student privacy comes first.

This initiative is being designed around school oversight, age-appropriate experiences, and strong privacy boundaries. The current site is informational only and does not collect student information.

  • ✓ Separate student platform and data environment
  • ✓ Private-by-default learning projects
  • ✓ Teacher- and school-guided workflows
  • ✓ No public student profiles or open messaging
  • ✓ Public sharing disabled until approvals are designed

FOR SCHOOLS & EDUCATORS
