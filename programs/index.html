Six Learning Pathways

A bigger world than podcasting.

The Academy uses media production as a practical way to build communication, research, creativity, collaboration, and technology skills. Students can explore one pathway or combine them in educator-approved projects.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Podcasting & Broadcasting

Develop hosting, interviewing, voice communication, audio production, show planning, and broadcast presentation skills.

Podcast format comparison · Interview planning · Educational audio episode

02 · Journalism & Newsroom

Learn reporting, research, source evaluation, fact-checking, news writing, community reporting, and responsible storytelling.

News brief · Source verification challenge · Community interview

03 · Film & Video Production

Explore camera operation, lighting, video recording, editing, documentaries, interviews, and visual storytelling.

Short documentary · How-to video · Interview feature

04 · Writing & Storytelling

Build scriptwriting, creative writing, narrative structure, dialogue, storytelling, and research-based writing skills.

Podcast script · Original audio story · Persuasive audio essay

05 · Digital Media & Design

Practice graphic design, thumbnails, visual communication, branding, digital publishing, and media technology.

Show identity kit · Episode artwork · Visual explainer

06 · Media Leadership & Production

Understand project management, production coordination, scheduling, team leadership, editorial decisions, and collaboration.

Production plan · Team role rotation · Project retrospective

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
