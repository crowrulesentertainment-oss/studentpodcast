Funding & Sustainability

A free educational experience supported by organizations.

CrowRules intends to sustain the Academy through a mix of corporate sponsorships, funded partnerships, eligible grants, commercial production services, workshops, events, and appropriate donations or underwriting.

Academy homeFor schoolsSupport the AcademyStudent-to-Creator

01 · Grants and funded partnerships

Explore media literacy, digital citizenship, arts education, career readiness, workforce development, and community education funding.

A school district or eligible organization may be the grant recipient; CrowRules may be a paid provider or subcontractor only where rules allow.

02 · Commercial production services

CrowRules can offer podcast setup, audio editing, episode production, promotional video, launch packages, and ongoing production services to paying organizations.

Commercial work is separate from student learning and must not compromise school program privacy.

03 · Educator workshops

Funded workshops may cover classroom podcasting, digital storytelling, journalism, media literacy, and audio/video editing.

Core Academy curriculum and student participation remain free.

04 · Student Media Awards

An annual awards showcase can be supported by presenting sponsors, category sponsors, event underwriting, and equipment donations.

No student entry fees or required purchases.

05 · Financial integrity

Track restricted funding, contracts, costs, sponsor commitments, and Academy allocations separately.

Do not describe payments to a commercial CrowRules business as tax-deductible charitable donations unless a qualified charitable recipient or fiscal sponsor is involved.

Always free for schools and students.

The Academy's educational experience is designed to remain free. CrowRules sustains the program through organizational support, sponsorships, funded partnerships, and separate commercial services. Student privacy, school oversight, and educational purpose remain central.
